FRC 2025

FIRST Robotics Competition · 2025 season

Designed approximately 60% of the robot, machined approximately 40% of its components, and completed approximately 40% of assembly.

Technical Highlights

  • The 26-by-26-inch sheet-metal chassis was designed for strength, low weight, a low center of gravity, and accessible wiring.
  • REV MAXSwerve modules provided a calculated top speed of 18.57 feet per second.
  • A three-stage cascading elevator used REV MAXTube joined with tube plugs and sheet-metal gussets.
  • Two NEO motors drove a jackshaft and chain system, while constant-force springs reduced the load on the elevator chains.
